
# Pit Stop Food & Merch Drop
**Order ahead. Pick up fast. Get back to the action.**

## Project Overview
**Pit Stop Food & Merch Drop** is a race-day ordering and collection concept designed to help fans purchase food, drinks, and merchandise without missing important on-track action.

Fans can place an order through the Grand Prix platform, select a nearby **Pit Stop Drop Point**, and receive a notification when their order is ready to collect. This reduces the need to walk long distances to food or merchandise areas and helps minimise time spent waiting in queues.

The concept also supports features such as:
-   **Pre-Orders** – fans can order ahead and choose a preferred collection time.
-   **Live Collection Updates** – estimated wait times can help fans choose a less busy Pit Stop.
-   **Nearby Pit Stop Selection** – fans can choose a convenient collection point based on their viewing location.
- **And Much More**

**The goal** is to improve the fan experience while also supporting increased food and merchandise sales, reducing congestion at major stores and food outlets, and distributing crowds across multiple collection points.
